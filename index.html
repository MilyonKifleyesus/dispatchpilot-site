What DispatchPilot does

DispatchPilot is a Chrome extension for dispatch teams. You pick the load, check the email and press Send. It goes out through your own Gmail, under your own name, and the reply comes back to your inbox.

Features

Three things it does well.

Your address on every email

Emails go out through your own Gmail account, so brokers see a name they know and replies land in your inbox.

Built for the whole team

Invite your dispatchers, approve who joins, and work the same board together.

Sends only what you ask

DispatchPilot does not read your inbox. It sends an email when you press Send, and at no other time.

How it works

On the road in three steps.

  1. Add DispatchPilot to Chrome

    Install the extension and sign in with Google. At sign-in we ask only for your name and email.

  2. Connect Gmail

    When you are ready to send, connect your Gmail. This is the only time we ask for permission to send email for you.

  3. Press Send

    Review the email, press Send, and it leaves from your own address.
