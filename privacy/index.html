PrivacyPolicy.

Last updated

November 16, 2026

DispatchPilot is a Chrome extension that helps dispatch teams send email from their own Gmail. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect your name and your email address. We use them to create your account and to identify you in DispatchPilot.

Connect Gmail

If you choose Connect Gmail, you give DispatchPilot permission to send email from your Gmail account. We use that permission only to send the emails you ask us to send.

We do not read your inbox.

What we do not do

  • We do not read your inbox.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

Google API data

DispatchPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting your data

You can remove DispatchPilot's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

To have your data deleted, email us at mili.kifleyesus@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to mili.kifleyesus@gmail.com.
